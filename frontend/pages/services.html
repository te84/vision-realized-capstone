The Process

1. Submit your Quote

Complete our online quote form and create an account to access your client dashboard.

2. Initial Meeting

Define event type and scope with you. We also discuss potential caterers, materials needed, renting event furniture, centerpieces, and other people to hire (photographers, DJs, live music, etc.)

3. You Choose your Options

We recieve quotes from potential venues, vendors, rental companies, and live music, and provide you 3 options for each event feature based on your budget. You choose what best fits your vision

4. We Book for You

Based on your choices, we negotiate with vendors and book sessions on your behalf.

5. Onsite Event Coordination and Management

We coordinate event setup and onsite event management the day of, so you can enjoy your event.

6. Payment

Your total bill will include our service as event planners, venue and vendor costs, and any external services
