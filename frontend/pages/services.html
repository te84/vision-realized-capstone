What We Cover

Event Conceptualization & Design

We work with you to develop a theme and creative direction that brings your vision to life — from color palettes and layout planning to hiring the right decorators and designers.

Logistics & Vendor Management

We source and coordinate vendors based on your needs and budget — negotiating contracts, managing deliveries, and keeping every moving piece on schedule so you don't have to.

Budget Planning & Financial Management

Transparent budget tracking so you know exactly where every dollar goes. Share your budget with us and we'll tailor every aspect of your event to fit — no surprises, no overspending.

The Process


1. Submit your Quote

Complete our online quote form and create an account to access your client dashboard.

2. Initial Meeting

We sit down with you to define the event type and scope. Together, we discuss potential caterers, materials, furniture rentals, centerpieces, and other vendors to hire — photographers, DJs, live music, and more.

3. You Choose your Options

We gather quotes from venues, vendors, rental companies, and entertainment, then present you with three options for each feature based on your budget. You choose what best fits your vision.

4. We Book for You

Based on your choices, we negotiate with vendors and book sessions on your behalf.

5. Onsite Event Coordination and Management

We coordinate event setup and onsite event management the day of, so you can enjoy your event.

6. Payment

Your total bill includes our event planning service, venue and vendor costs, and any external services — all clearly outlined with no hidden fees.
